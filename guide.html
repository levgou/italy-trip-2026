🏝 Borromean Islands

Isola Bella SHOWSTOPPER
Baroque palace + terraced gardens. ~1.5h visit. Maps →
Isola dei Pescatori
Tiny fishing village. Eat lunch here — whole island is restaurants. Maps →

🍝 Where to Eat

🍷 La Rampolina ~25–35 EUR/pp TOP PICK
Terrace overlooking lake + Borromean Islands. Locals' favorite. Piemontese + lake fish. Maps →
🍝 Trattoria Lo Stornello ~20–30 EUR/pp
Simple family spot, centro storico. Order: pesce persico, risotto, stufato. Maps →
🍷 Osteria degli Amici ~20–30 EUR/pp
Homey, daily menu. Zuppe, spezzatini, paste fresche. Maps →

🍦 Gelato

🍦 L'Angolo del Gelato ~3–5 EUR
Centro Stresa. Try caramello salato. Maps →

🛏 Where to Sleep

🌾 Agriturismo Nel Giardino di Dafne ~100–130 EUR
Breakfast basket at 8am: local milk, yogurt, fresh pastries. Quiet garden. Maps →
🏨 Hotel La Palma ~120–150 EUR
4-star on lungolago. Rooftop terrace sunset panorama. Maps →

🌙 Evening · 🏖 Swim · ☂ Rain

🌙 Stresa quiet at night. Lungolago passeggiata. Aperitivo on hotel terrace, sunset over islands.

🏖 Spiaggia Lido di Stresa — pebbly beach in town. Water ~18–19°C end of May.

☂ Isola Bella palace is indoor. Or drive to Borromeo Castle in Angera (30 min).
☂ Rocca di Angera (rain backup)
Medieval castle, doll museum, lake views from tower. 30 min from Stresa.
