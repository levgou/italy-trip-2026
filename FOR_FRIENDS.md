## The A/B fork logic (so you can advise)

Each lettered day is a real choice, not cascading:
- **Day 3:** Monte Baldo (active, cable car to 1760m, big views) **vs** Sirmione thermal swim (chill, Roman ruins + Jamaica Beach hot springs).
- **Day 4:** cliff-terrace lunch + short Ponale **vs** quick lunch + full Ponale hike.
- **Day 5 + 6 pairing tip:** don't stack two hard days. Best combos: **A5+A6** (hike Brenta, chill Braies) or **B5+B6** (chill, then Tre Cime). "Hard" = real hiking (Rifugio Croz 3h/+400m, or Tre Cime walk at 2300m). "Easy" = cable car + flat strolls + lake.
- **Day 8:** flight is the hard constraint (MXP 20:00). Plan A = relaxed. Plan B = quick Verona coffee/Arena-exterior only, bail if running late.
